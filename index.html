Dr. Andrews Korah

Postdoctoral Research Associate, University of Oklahoma

Dr. Andrews Korah holds a Ph.D. in Geography and Environmental Sustainability from the University of Oklahoma and an M.A. in Geography from Miami University.

As a highly skilled spatial data scientist, his expertise encompasses R, Python, and Google Earth Engine. He leverages these computational tools to map, model, and dissect complex urban environments and microclimates. Beyond his academic research, Dr. Korah brings extensive applied experience, serving as a consultant for the World Bank Group's City Resilience Program, helping cities build resilience against climate-induced hazards and extreme heat events.

In addition to his research and consultancy, Dr. Korah is dedicated to mentoring the next generation of spatial data scientists. At the University of Oklahoma, his teaching portfolio spans both graduate and undergraduate levels, including courses such as GIS 5453: Advanced GIS and Spatial Analysis, GEOG 1114: Physical Geography, and GEOG 2603: World Regional Geography.
